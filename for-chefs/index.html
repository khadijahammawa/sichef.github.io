GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

How do I join?

Complete our chef signup form and tell us a little about your cooking. Our team will get in touch within 24–48 hours with the next steps to access the chef app and set up your profile.

Can I choose when I cook?

Absolutely—you’re in control of your schedule. Whether you offer occasional pickups or take pre-orders for a planned menu, you choose what to cook, how many portions to offer and when customers can collect. Cook as often as you like, whenever it suits you.

How do payments work?

Payments are securely processed through Stripe, with payouts sent to your connected bank account according to your payout schedule. Arrival times may vary, and your first payout can take longer.

During our early launch phase, SíChef takes no commission from chefs. Payment processing fees may apply. For help with payments or getting started, contact hello@sichef.es.
