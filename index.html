OUR STORY

A city full of kitchens. A reason to connect.

We’re Khadija and Norika, the co-founders of SíChef. We’re building a place for Barcelona’s independent chefs and food lovers to find each other.

Our idea is simple. Make it easier to discover the cooking, people and stories in your neighbourhood.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled through Stripe in the ordering app.

Where do I pick up my food?

Check the pickup location and available times in the ordering app before placing your order.

Have another question?
